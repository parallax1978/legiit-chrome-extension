About BrightPath Dental

Dr. Elena Vargas opened BrightPath Dental in 2014 with a simple idea: give every patient enough time. We are a small, independent practice, not part of a chain.

Our team of two dentists and four hygienists lives in the neighborhoods we serve. We keep our schedule light on purpose, so appointments start on time and nobody is hurried out of the chair.

Our promise

You will always see the price before we begin. If we would not recommend a treatment to our own family, we will not recommend it to you.
