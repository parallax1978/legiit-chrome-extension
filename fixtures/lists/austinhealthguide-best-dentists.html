10 Best Dentists in Austin (2026)

By Maya Ortiz · Updated March 2026

Finding a dentist you trust takes time. Our editors compared patient reviews, published pricing and appointment availability across more than sixty practices to rank the ten best dentists in Austin this year.

Every practice on this list accepts new patients and publishes its fees for a standard cleaning and exam.

1. Lakeside Smiles

Lakeside Smiles is praised for gentle cleanings, same-week appointments and clear pricing for new patients. The office on Lakeshore Boulevard opens early on weekdays.

2. Capitol Dental Care

Capitol Dental Care is a downtown practice known for cosmetic work and evening hours, which makes it a favourite with people who work near the Capitol.

3. Zilker Family Dentistry

Zilker Family Dentistry is a popular choice for families with young children, with a play corner and patient hygienists.

4. Barton Creek Dental Studio

A small studio with a strong reputation for crowns and implants.

5. Mueller Modern Dentistry

A newer office in the Mueller district with online booking.

6. Hyde Park Dental Arts

Long-established practice with many second-generation patients.

7. East Side Smile Co.

Affordable cleanings and Spanish-speaking staff.

8. Domain Dental Group

Large group practice with weekend hours.

9. Travis Heights Dentistry

Known for calm treatment of anxious patients.

10. Riverside Family Dental

Good value for families without insurance.

How we chose

We weighted patient reviews at fifty percent, pricing transparency at thirty percent and availability at twenty percent. Practices cannot pay to be included.
