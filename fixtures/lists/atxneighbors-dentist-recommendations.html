Dentist recommendations from Austin neighbors

We asked our members which dentist they would recommend to a new neighbor. Two names came up more than any other.

Neighbors in South Austin recommend BrightPath Dental for gentle care and for hygienists who never rush an appointment.

North of the river, Lakeside Smiles was the most common answer, mostly for its early opening hours.
