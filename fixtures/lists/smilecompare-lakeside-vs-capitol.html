Lakeside Smiles vs Capitol Dental Care: Which Austin Dentist Is Better?

Lakeside Smiles and Capitol Dental Care are the two most reviewed dental practices in Austin. We compared them on price, hours, services and patient feedback.

Price

Lakeside Smiles offers a new patient special, while Capitol Dental Care charges a little more but includes X-rays in its exam fee.

Hours

Capitol Dental Care is a downtown practice known for cosmetic work and evening hours. Lakeside Smiles opens early and closes at five.

Verdict

Choose Lakeside Smiles for routine family care and Capitol Dental Care for cosmetic treatment.
