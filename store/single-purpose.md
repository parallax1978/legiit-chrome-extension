# Single purpose

For the Developer Dashboard → Privacy practices → "Single purpose description".

## Statement

```
Shows how visible a web page or brand is in Google results and AI answers, and what explains it.
```

## Supporting text

Paste under the statement if the field allows more, or keep for a reviewer's question.

```
Legiit Lens is an SEO analysis tool. Every feature answers the same question for the page or brand the user is looking at: how visible is it in search results and AI answers, and why?

- Click Potential measures a Google results page the user opened: how much of the first screen organic results get.
- Why Not Me shows on which parts of a results page or AI answer the user's brand is missing while competitors are present, and reads the cited pages to explain it.
- Rewind shows what changed on a page over time (from the Wayback Machine), to explain a change in visibility.
- New or Noise compares a page's sentences with the pages it competes against, to explain why it does or does not stand out.
- Instant Brief collects the same findings for one business website into a report for a client.

The extension does not change search results, the new tab page or the browser's settings, shows no ads, and has no functionality outside this purpose.
```

## Why it is one purpose and not a bundle

The five tabs share one input (a page or brand the user is looking at), one set of tracked brands, and one output (a visibility finding with its explanation). They reuse each other's data: Instant Brief's "Search presence" section is built from Click Potential and Why Not Me results. None of them is useful for anything other than search-visibility analysis.
