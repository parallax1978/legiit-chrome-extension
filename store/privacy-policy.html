Legiit Lens Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE — placeholder]

Legiit Lens is a Chrome extension published by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME — placeholder] ("Legiit", "we"). It shows how visible a web page or brand is in Google results and AI answers, and what explains it.

This policy describes what the extension reads, what it stores, and what leaves your device.

The short version

  • To start, you enter your email address. It is sent to Legiit, which emails you a code that connects your free Legiit account. Nothing you analyze is sent to us unless you choose to send it.
  • It reads only pages you open or ask it to read. It never sends searches to Google or prompts to AI sites.
  • Your tracked brands, history and briefs are stored on your device.
  • There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking in the extension.
  • Data you analyze is sent to Legiit only when you click a save or AI action, and you are shown exactly what will be sent before it goes.

What the extension reads

Google results pages you open. On Google Search pages the extension reads the results on your screen: the position and size of ads, AI Overviews, local results and organic results, their titles and addresses, your search keyword, and the place name Google prints on the page. It shows a summary strip above the results.

AI answers you open. On chatgpt.com, gemini.google.com, perplexity.ai and copilot.microsoft.com, when you open the Legiit Lens panel, it reads your latest question, the answer, and the links the answer cites.

Other pages, only when you ask. On any other site the extension has no access until you click its toolbar icon, use its keyboard shortcut, or choose it from the right-click menu on that page. It then reads that page's text, links, headings and styling. Access ends when the tab leaves that site.

Pages you choose to scan or compare. Two features read further pages: "Scan sources" reads pages cited by a results page or AI answer, and "New or Noise" reads pages that compete with yours. Before either runs, Chrome asks you to allow each site by name. Your browser fetches those pages without sending cookies. "Instant Brief" reads up to eight pages of the business website you are on.

Archived copies of a page. When you look up a page's history, the extension requests archived copies of that page from the Internet Archive (web.archive.org).

What is stored on your device

  • Settings: your brand and competitor names and domains, your agency name and logo, and your preferences.
  • Snapshots of Google results pages: keyword, date and time, window size, the place name shown by Google, and where each result appeared. A snapshot is saved when you view a results page with the panel open, or when you press Save. You can change this in Settings.
  • Client briefs you build.
  • Caches of pages and archive captures the extension fetched, so repeat lookups are fast.
  • Your Legiit API token, which connects the extension to your account.

All of this is kept in your browser's storage for the extension. It is not synced to your Google account and is not readable by websites. You can delete it at any time in Settings → Privacy and data ("Clear history", "Clear caches", "Erase everything"), or by removing the extension.

What leaves your device

Your email address, and the code you type. When you click Start on the first screen, the extension sends the email address you entered to Legiit. Legiit keeps it and emails you a 6-digit code. When you type that code into the extension, it is sent to Legiit, which creates your free Legiit account (or finds the one your address already has) and returns a token that connects the extension to it. Legiit may email you about your account.

Other websites. Besides Legiit, the extension contacts only:

  • web.archive.org, when you look up a page's history. The request contains the address of the page you are looking up. The Internet Archive's own privacy policy applies to that request.
  • Websites you allowed for a scan or comparison, and the website you build a brief on. These receive ordinary page requests from your browser, without cookies and without a referrer. They can see your IP address, as with any visit.

Legiit, with your account:

  • The extension reads your own account data from Legiit (your name, plan, businesses, their tracked competitors, focus keywords and customer profiles) to pre-fill its settings, and Legiit's list of notable search-industry dates.
  • When you click Save to Command Center, Send to content editor, Open in Command Center, Add to Command Center or Rewrite with Legiit AI, the extension shows the exact data it will send and sends it only after you confirm. Depending on the action this can include: a search keyword and the results measured on that page; a list of pages, the brands they mention and the contact links found on those pages; sentences from a page you analyzed; or a client brief with the business details found on a website.
  • Your API token and the extension's version number are sent to Legiit with each of these requests, and to no one else. A save also carries a random one-time number, so that sending it twice by accident does not store it twice.

Data you save to your Legiit account is handled under Legiit's privacy policy: [LINK TO LEGIIT PRIVACY POLICY — placeholder].

You can disconnect at any time in Settings. Disconnecting deletes the token and everything that was pre-filled from your account from the extension.

On-device AI

"New or Noise" compares sentences using a small language model that is included in the extension and runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded for this.

If you opt in to Chrome's built-in AI in Settings, summaries can be rewritten by a model that Chrome runs on your device. Nothing is sent to us or, according to Google, to Google for this.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use or transfer your data for advertising.
  • We do not use or transfer your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • We do not collect analytics or usage statistics from the extension.
  • We do not read your browsing history. The extension keeps no record of the pages you visit other than the snapshots, briefs and caches described above, which stay on your device.

Limited Use

Legiit Lens's use of information it reads or receives complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
